# VERIFY

Resolve the specific contradiction or ambiguity in the assignment. Identify the
competing claims and evidence needed to distinguish them; do not audit every
earlier finding by default. Prefer a local integrity audit of an original saved
capture when that answers the question. Cite the original result_id in probe,
artifact_ref and exact quote from original/validated decoded bytes. A local
audit validates the quotation and provenance, not a new observation or its
interpretation. If necessary evidence is absent, state the gap or perform one
permitted distinguishing check. A flag proposal identifies objective_key, exact
value and available source/material references. Platform acceptance is decided
by the operator; VERIFY, a new capture or another network request is not required.
Model agreement and repeated output are not an operator answer.
